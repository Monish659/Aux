About the creator

Who built this?+
My name is Monish Reddy. I'm a rising junior and a high school student in Pleasanton, California. I built this entirely on my own, with no formal teaching background, just direct experience taking these exact courses and going deep until everything clicked. I also tutor on Schoolhouse.world, a free nonprofit peer-tutoring platform, where I have verified reviews from students I've worked with.
Are you a teacher? Do you have formal credentials?+
No formal credentials. I'm a student, not a teacher. Everything here comes from taking these exact classes myself, earning straight A's across all of them, and figuring out what actually needs to click for the material to make sense. That student perspective is the point. I know where it gets hard because I was there.
Why did you build this?+
I believe every student deserves a real shot at understanding the material, not just the ones who can afford a tutor or happen to have a great class. Once I worked through these courses and figured out what it actually takes, putting it somewhere anyone could access it felt like the obvious thing to do. Equal access matters.

About the content

Is it really free?+
Yes, completely. No login, no paywall, no hidden cost anywhere on the site. The videos, problem sets, and guides are all free and always will be. This site is funded by my own time, not by ads or sponsorships. The only optional thing is a $30 donation to the Pleasanton Library if you book a 1-on-1 session, and even that is not required.
What courses are covered?+
Physics is fully live right now, covering all 9 units from Units and Conversions through Waves, built for Honors, AP Physics 1, and AP Physics 2. Honors Precalculus is currently in production. More subjects are on the way. Everything I've taken and earned straight A's in is a candidate for this site.
What about 1-on-1 sessions?+
Optional live sessions are available separately from the free content: 60 minutes, your question, my full attention. A $30 donation to the Pleasanton Library is strongly encouraged as a way to give back to the community, but the session itself is free regardless. Book a session →
