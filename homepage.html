Why this exists

Bad teachers don't just frustrate you.
They cost you.

I watched it happen — classmates who understood the concepts, who put in the work, walking out with grades that had nothing to do with how capable they were. The teaching wasn't there. The support wasn't there. And the stress was everywhere.

I made it through with all As. Not because I'm exceptional — because I refused to let a bad teacher be my ceiling. I taught myself. I figured out the patterns, the traps, the exact questions these teachers weaponize against unprepared students. That knowledge belongs to everyone.

AUX is free, it will stay free, and it will keep growing. No student should ever be locked out of understanding material they were already owed.
