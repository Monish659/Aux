⚡ Test Prep

The question types that actually show up.

These problems show up on every test. They are not in most textbooks. I tracked the patterns. Here are the walkthroughs.

Multi-step Projectile
Launching from a height with non-standard angles — the ones that need two phases to solve.
Atwood's Machine Variations
Pulley problems with added friction, inclines, or three-mass systems.
Energy with Friction Path
Conservation of energy when friction is doing work — the part that gets left out of most notes.
Wave Superposition
Constructive and destructive interference drawn and calculated at the same time.
